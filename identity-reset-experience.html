About the experience

For people who know something has to break, but not yet what has been breaking them

The Identity Audit reveals. The Identity Reset Experience responds. It takes what has been surfaced and puts the participant into a more disciplined confrontation with the false patterns that keep draining peace, shrinking authority, and delaying movement.

You may purchase this experience before taking the Identity Audit. What you may not do is book the included coaching session before completing the Identity Audit and Days 1 and 2 of the reset. That rule stays because purchase alone is not proof of readiness.

The reset sequence

Ten days. Ten pressure points.

Each day targets a specific internal pressure point. The point is not inspiration. The point is interruption, naming, truth, and movement.
01
Overfunctioning
Expose the performance reflex that keeps you carrying what was never assigned to you.
02
Scarcity
Confront the agreement that says there is never enough safety, time, provision, or room.
03
Self-Doubt
Name the internal argument that has kept interrupting your movement and authority.
04
Fear of Visibility
Deal with the shrinking instinct that treats being seen like danger.
05
Imposter Syndrome
Break the lie that says you may enter the room but you do not truly belong in it.
06
Emotional Overload
Separate true feeling from unmanaged flooding, overwhelm, and collapse.
07
Fear of Failure
Expose the bargain you have been making with caution, hesitation, and self-protection.
08
People-Pleasing
Bring false peacekeeping and over-adaptation into the light and call it by name.
09
Delay
Treat postponement as agreement, not just personality, and interrupt its logic.
10
Smallness
Break the internal limit that keeps you negotiating against your own call and authority.
