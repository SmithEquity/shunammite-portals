What this experience does

Awareness is useful. A reset requires a decision.

The Identity Reset Experience™ turns reflection into a defined personal practice. You will not be told who you are or given a clinical label. You will examine what you have been accepting, decide what no longer aligns, and build a practical response anchored in truth.

01 / RECOGNIZE

Notice the influence

Identify recurring messages, labels, expectations, and assumptions that may be shaping a current choice or response.

02 / RESET

Test the agreement

Separate what is true from what has merely become familiar, then write a more aligned governing statement.

03 / PRACTICE

Build the response

Choose repeatable actions that help you notice, interrupt, replace, and practice the reset after the five days end.

The five-day sequence

Five disciplined moves. One question underneath them.

Each day builds on the one before it, moving from recognition to a defined personal practice without assigning a clinical label or prescribing a single answer. The full experience requires approximately three hours of participant work.

Day 1 · 25–30 min

Notice

Choose one present-life area and observe the messages and expectations influencing it.

Day 2 · 25–35 min

Name

Put clear language around the agreement or assumption you have been following.

Day 3 · 30–40 min

Challenge

Question its authority and compare it with truth, evidence, values, and faith.

Day 4 · 25–35 min

Choose

Define a different response, boundary, or action for the moment the old pattern appears.

Day 5 · 35–45 min

Establish

Complete your reset statement and prepare the personal practice that follows.

This is not another program phase or a promise of transformation. It is a participant-owned plan for practicing the decision made during the five-day experience.

The plan becomes available only after the Day 5 completion checkpoint.

Four practice movements

  1. Notice: recognize when the former agreement is influencing a response.
  2. Interrupt: pause before automatically repeating the familiar choice.
  3. Replace: return to the participant’s written reset statement and selected truth.
  4. Practice: take the defined aligned action and record what happened.
