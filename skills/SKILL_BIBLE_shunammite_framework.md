# SKILL BIBLE: The Shunammite Framework

**Source:** Live portal pages in this repo (book-kelly-v2.html, identity-audit-funnel-v2.html, becoming-whole-90day.html), extracted 2026-07-07.
**Use when:** writing any content, program material, or sales copy — this is the framework behind all of Kelly's work.

## Core model: Expansion · Elevation · Grace for Execution

A Kingdom framework for transformation that moves people from brokenness to rebuilding, and from rebuilding to purposeful habitation.

1. **Expansion** — Learning of Him; allowing correction, instruction, and rebuke in righteousness. Shifting everything through the Word. Laying down old thinking and intentionally choosing to believe according to His Word.
2. **Elevation** — Becoming an effective co-laborer. Applying the Word to your life and being led by Holy Spirit — doing only what you've seen your Father do.
3. **Grace for Execution** — Holy Spirit gives the increase: greater authority and anointings as you show yourself faithful. "He who began a good work in you is faithful to complete it."

## The belief chain (the diagnostic logic)

Beliefs → thoughts → feelings → choices → patterns. Lasting change starts in beliefs, not effort. Key line: "Most people try to change by trying harder. Lasting change starts deeper — in beliefs."

Full loop taught in the audit: **Trigger → Reaction → Payoff → Belief → Truth → New Choice.**

## The four rooms (Identity Audit structure)

1. **Performance & Control** — what do you rely on to feel safe that is actually controlling your life? Coping mechanisms offered: proving worth through achievement/output/being needed; managing environment/people/outcomes; avoidance-driven decisions; withdrawal; distraction.
2. **What your behavior says about God** — where behavior contradicts stated belief. "Your actions speak louder than your theology."
3. **Labels** — what identity have you accepted and still act from, and what benefit do you get from keeping it?
4. **The root belief** — the belief you are protecting even though it is costing you.

## The identity patterns (audit results)

- **The Performer** — worth tied to output; rest feels dangerous, stillness feels like failure.
- **The Orphan/Uncovered** — operating from a sense of being on your own, even in community.
- **The Wounded** — past injury has become present introduction.
- **The Cycle-Repeater** — sees the cycle but keeps repeating it even while hating it.
