## Hard gating rules (never write copy that contradicts these)

1. The Identity Reset **included coaching session** unlocks only after the Identity Audit AND Days 1–2 of the Reset are complete. "Purchase alone is not proof of readiness."
2. The included session and the paid Embodiment Session are **not the same thing**.
3. All booking flows through Book Kelly once the unlock condition is met.
4. 90-Day Pathway modules unlock on a disciplined weekly schedule — "self-service with disciplined pacing," never "go at your own pace."
5. You may buy the Reset before the audit — but the session gate still stands. Two entry points, one standard.

- **Phase II · Days 31–60** — internal reconstruction; dismantle old thought systems, rebuild through truth, language, aligned repetition. Ends with Session #2: **Recalibrate**.

## Identity Reset — the 10 pressure points (Days 1–10)

1. Performance reflex (carrying what was never assigned)
2. Scarcity agreement (never enough safety/time/provision/room)
3. The internal argument interrupting movement and authority
4. The shrinking instinct (being seen feels like danger)
5. Belonging lie (in the room but not of it)
6. Feeling vs. flooding (overwhelm, collapse)
7. The bargain with caution and self-protection
8. False peacekeeping and over-adaptation
9. Postponement as agreement, not personality
10. The internal limit negotiating against your own call
