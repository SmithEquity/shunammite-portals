About the Pathway

For People Tired of Living Outwardly Functional
but Inwardly Fragmented

The Becoming Whole Transformation Pathway is a structured, 90-day focus designed to move individuals from identity confusion and "gutted" areas of life into a state of stability and Kingdom purpose.

This is not self-paced whenever you want. It is self-service with disciplined pacing — a guided process that combines video teaching, audio reflections, workbook exercises, and three private strategic sessions with Kelly. Each week builds on the last. Each phase gates the next. You cannot skip the process, but you can trust it.

The 90 days are structured as three 30-day phases with 12 core teaching modules. Move week by week. Do the work. Leave changed.
