Who Were You Before the World Told You Who to Be?

The Identity Audit™ is a free, faith-based guided reflection designed to help you examine beliefs, experiences, labels, expectations, and recurring patterns that may be influencing how you see yourself and how you move through your life.

This Audit will not tell you who you are. It gives you structured questions to help you notice what may deserve a closer look.

Step 2 — Quick Scan

What Are You Noticing Right Now?

Do not overthink these questions. Choose what feels most accurate based on your experience right now.

When something goes wrong, what is your first internal response?

When you feel uncertain or unsafe, what do you tend to rely on most?

There is no “right” answer. You are simply noticing what you tend to reach for.

Step 3 — Beliefs

What Have You Come to Believe?

Think about a situation, relationship, role, or area of life that has been difficult or repeatedly frustrating.

You are not being asked to prove that this belief is the cause of anything. You are simply examining whether there may be a connection worth noticing.

Step 4 — Performance, Control & Protection

What Do You Reach For?

When life feels uncertain, demanding, or uncomfortable, consider what you tend to reach for.

Step 5 — Your View of God

What Do You Believe About God—and What Do You Expect From Him?

What we say we believe and what we expect when life becomes difficult are not always the same.

This is not a test of your faith. It is an invitation to notice whether there is anything you want to understand more deeply.

Step 6 — Labels

What Have You Been Called—or Come to Call Yourself?

Some labels may describe a season. Some describe a role. Some may never have belonged to us at all.

Step 7 — Reflection Check

Looking Across Your Answers, What Seems to Deserve Your Attention?

You do not need to define yourself or figure everything out here. Simply notice what appears to be influencing how you think, respond, make decisions, relate to others, or see yourself.
