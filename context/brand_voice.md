# Brand Voice — Shunammite Enterprises / Kelly E. Smith

*Last updated: 2026-07-07. Derived from live portal copy. Refine with Kelly's corrections — the corrections are where the real value gets encoded.*

## Tone

Direct, confrontational-but-loving, pastoral authority. The voice names hard things plainly and refuses to soften diagnosis into flattery. It challenges the reader to stop settling, then immediately offers a structured path. Faith language is native, not decorative: Kingdom, Holy Spirit, the Word, commissioning, anointing.

## Signature moves

- **Short declarative verdicts.** "This is not a quiz. This is a mirror." / "Purchase alone is not proof of readiness." / "You cannot skip the process, but you can trust it."
- **Name-the-thing constructions.** "The root you refuse to name will keep bearing the same fruit." Naming, exposure, and interruption are recurring verbs.
- **Discipline framed as care.** Gates and rules are presented as protection, not friction ("That rule stays because purchase alone is not proof of readiness").
- **Triads.** Identity · Healing · Freedom. Expansion · Elevation · Grace for Execution. Diagnose / Recalibrate / Commission.
- **Second person, present tense.** "You leave Phase I knowing where you are gutted."

## Words we use

Wholeness, identity, root, pattern, naming, exposure, interruption, alignment, authority, stewardship, commissioning, stabilization, embodiment, gutted, fractured, agreement (as in "living under agreements").

## Words we ban

- "Journaling prompts," "self-care," "manifest/manifestation," "universe" (New Age register — this is Kingdom language, not wellness language).
- "Life-changing," "unlock your best life," and other generic coach-speak superlatives.
- Clinical/therapy claims: never "therapy," "treatment," "diagnosis" in the medical sense — the disclaimer says educational, faith-based content only.
- Hype pricing language: "insane value," "steal," countdown-timer urgency.

## Formatting rules

- Headlines can be blunt fragments. Body copy is complete sentences.
- Testimonials always carry a real attribution (first name + last initial) — never invented.
- Prices stated plainly, once, near the CTA. No strikethrough fake-discount pricing.

## One good example (from the live pages)

> "The Identity Reset Experience is the bridge between revelation and reconstruction. It is built for the person who is outwardly functioning but inwardly fragmented, overextended, misaligned, or living under agreements that keep shrinking truth, authority, and peace."

## One bad example (what we never sound like)

> "Ready to unlock your BEST life?! 🚀 This life-changing program is packed with insane value — grab it before the timer runs out!"
