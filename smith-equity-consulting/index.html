Total Rewards Architecture

Global Equity Compliance

Navigate the cross-border complexity of international equity programs — from securities law to country-specific tax treatment and subplan design.

  • Country-by-country compliance analysis
  • Securities registration and exemption guidance

  • Mobile employee sourcing and reporting
  • U.S. citizen worldwide income reporting considerations
ASC 718 & IFRS 2 Compliance

How We Work

A Deliberate Engagement
Process

Every engagement begins with understanding what you have, identifying what is missing, and building what you need — in that order.

Step One

Discovery & Diagnostic

We start by mapping your current program — what exists, what is working, where the risk and compliance gaps are, and what the business is trying to accomplish. No assumptions, no templates applied before understanding your situation.

Step Two

Design & Documentation

Based on the diagnostic, we develop the program design, governance framework, and documentation your organization needs. Every recommendation is grounded in what your specific company, stage, and workforce requires — not what another client used.

Step Three

Implementation & Ongoing Support

We support execution through to completion and remain available as your program evolves. Compensation programs are living documents — new grants, new geographies, new leadership, new regulations all create follow-on work that we handle as your ongoing advisor.
